Two approaches, one idea
Simple recursion
Count one square (+1), then solve the smaller rectangle. Correct idea, but may make millions of calls.
1 square per callCan be too slow
Optimized recursion
Count every same-size square at once with division, then use modulo to find the leftover rectangle.
quotient = squaresremainder = leftover
Core intuition: don't change what the function is solving. Just count all identical squares in one step instead of counting them one by one.
